Main VOLLEY and VOLLEY-lab preserve release snapshots of work completed by the Friday cutoff. Subsequent innovations receive separate versions, commits and releases. They do not silently modify the frozen academic edition. A repository freeze preserves access and history; it does not archive the repository or disable access.

After freeze, a correction is published as an identified erratum or replacement release. An erratum names the affected source version and artifact hash, explains the correction and its effect on results, and links the verified replacement when applicable. Preserve the original submitted and released artifacts. Material engineering corrections require the affected evidence and dependent documents to be rebuilt and checked before a replacement is issued.

Actual similarity feedback, signatures, institutional approvals and presentation/rehearsal evidence retain their genuine dates and outcomes. A scheduled freeze does not supply those external records or establish that all criteria are complete.
