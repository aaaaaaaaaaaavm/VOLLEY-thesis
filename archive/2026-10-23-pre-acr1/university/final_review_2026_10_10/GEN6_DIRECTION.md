## Selected feeder — 23 October 2026

The academic feeder design baseline is **FM08-R3**, an independently actuated guided lead-screw cassette architecture with fixed 1U spacers and a common indexed receiver. The [local selected-design package](../../validation/selected_feeder/README.md) supplies the current decision and evidence boundary. The earlier architecture records below remain dated research history. This design selection is separate from physical qualification and from the 1 km/s research objective.

## Mission and investigation envelope

The host performs coarse orbital repositioning; VOLLEY aims to provide individually commanded release conditions to sequential CubeSats of different supported sizes. The controlling Gen6 objective is one reusable launch path with sequential loading. The approximate 1–2 m/s low end through 1 km/s high end is an **architecture-trade investigation envelope**. It is not a selected operating specification, a demonstrated speed, evidence of payload tolerance, or provider accommodation.

Electromagnetic drive is preferred for comparison but is not preselected. The trade includes shared electromagnetic LSM/LIM guides, other electromagnetic drives, electromechanical and stored-energy systems, gas/pneumatic/hydraulic systems, hybrids, banked paths, historical VOLLEY concepts, and conventional deployment/mission substitutes.

## Evidence gates

1. **N0:** Freeze named mission, payload and host envelopes, release accuracy, contact/acceleration limits and acceptance bands.
2. **N1:** Produce coupled multi-payload mission evidence with uncertainties, host resources and a conventional comparison.
3. **N2:** Attempt to reject candidate mechanisms under the same mission and installed-system boundary, including loading, retention, arrest, reset, jam recovery and control.
4. **N3:** Close mass, volume, energy, thermal, fault and scaling burden for competing paths and a conventional baseline.
5. **Electrical implementation:** Close the selected VOLLEY accelerator/source/circuit/thermal evidence on its own configuration; no side-project gate is a VOLLEY dependency.
6. **N5:** Produce the first-test evidence package with provisional geometry, BOM, assembly/inspection, instrumentation, calibration, uncertainty, acceptance and stop criteria.

The agreed near-term end goal is one named, reproducible, independently reviewable test-article configuration with an explicit build/test release disposition. N5 alone does not establish final build/test release. Manufacturing, physical tests, environmental qualification, provider/customer integration and flight demonstration are later work.

The active status of every gate must be reconciled with actual task and artifact evidence before anyone marks it complete. The plan baseline is a source for scope and sequence, not independent engineering verification.

## Expanded development studies

Four- and twelve-payload studies, all seven feeder candidates, and lunar/high-speed computational investigations are requested development scope. They are not completed Gen5 results. Reuse within one loaded campaign is the reference; orbital replenishment is a separate feasibility/interface concept. No physical build, provider acceptance or reload demonstration is established.
