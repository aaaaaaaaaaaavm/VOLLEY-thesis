# A34 — historical scalar-restitution limit

**The former zero-release-rate conclusion is withdrawn as a closure claim.** A scalar bounce-settling model omits compliant contact energy, preload, friction and three-dimensional force sharing. Settling in that model cannot establish zero tip-off, reliable release or safe operation without preload.

The historical calculation is a model result only. A selected contact geometry, measured contact law, nonlinear dynamic cross-check and instrumented release are required before assigning a release-quality limit. [Uncertainty and fault interpretation](../docs/GEN5_UNCERTAINTY_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md) retains this unresolved behavior. No hardware result is implied.
